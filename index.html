About

An independent plugin studio

Folix Audio has started. We're an independent studio making synthesizers and effects for producers and sound designers who want to go somewhere new.

Folix is an invented word, part foliage, part helix: sound that grows and spirals outward, like a nebula curling into a new star.

Folix Audio has five plugins available now: Euphora, Drop 2, EQ Follower, Delay and Shape EQ.
