Overview

What it does

Euphora is a multiband mastering dynamics plugin for your master bus or stems. Split the mix into two or three bands, give each its own compressor with threshold, ratio, attack, release, knee and makeup, and solo or bypass any band to hear exactly what it is doing.

After the bands, a color stage adds warm saturation, and the limiter with soft clip sets your ceiling. A live spectrum, short-term LUFS readout and input/output and gain-reduction meters show where your master sits, so you can push it loud without crushing it.

Features

Key features

  • 2- or 3-band compression with adjustable crossovers, solo and bypass per band
  • Threshold, ratio, attack, release, knee and makeup on every band
  • Color stage with drive and selectable character for warmth and glue
  • Limiter with soft clip and ceiling control, plus LUFS loudness and gain-reduction metering
  • Factory presets for mastering, such as Loud EDM
