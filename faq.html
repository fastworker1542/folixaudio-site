Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about Folix Audio plugins.

When do the plugins launch?

Five plugins are available now: Euphora, Drop 2, EQ Follower, Delay and Shape EQ. Release news goes to the mailing list first.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Free Windows and Mac demos for Euphora and Drop 2 are available now. They are fully working, but mute audio for 1 second every 45 seconds. Demos for EQ Follower, Delay and Shape EQ are coming soon.

What formats are supported?

Euphora and Drop 2: VST3 and AU on Windows and macOS. EQ Follower and Shape EQ: VST3 on Windows 10/11 (64-bit); Mac coming. Delay: VST3 on Windows, plus AU, VST3 and Standalone on Mac. Full details are on each product page.

How much do they cost?

Euphora and Drop 2 are $40 each. EQ Follower, Delay and Shape EQ are $29.99 each. Buy on each product page (Gumroad checkout).

What's the refund policy?

Purchases can be refunded within 14 days of purchase. Email folixaudio@gmail.com with your order details, or see the refund policy. Where a free demo is available, please try it first.

I need help. Who do I contact?

Email folixaudio@gmail.com or use the contact form.
