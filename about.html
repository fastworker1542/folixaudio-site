About

Where new sounds are born

Folix Audio is an independent studio building synthesizers and effects for producers and sound designers.

Our story

The story so far

Folix Audio started the way most plugins do: with a sound that didn't exist yet. Instead of waiting for someone else to build it, we wrote the code ourselves and kept going until it sounded right.

Folix is a made-up word. We hear foliage and helix in it: something organic that keeps growing, twisting into shapes you didn't plan. That's the idea behind every plugin we make, and it's why our motto is simple: shape the unknown.

Available now: Euphora, a multiband mastering dynamics plugin with per-band compression, color and a limiter; and Drop 2, a sub-bass instrument for fat, lush basslines and deep sub. Both have free Windows and Mac demos. Also available now: EQ Follower, a pitch-tracking EQ whose bands ride the note; Delay, a delay built like a reverb; and Shape EQ, two EQ curves with a morph. Falling Waves and Backwash are in development and coming later; join the mailing list for release news.
